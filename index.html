Keep both devices online and preferably on the same Wi-Fi. Camera video is sent directly between the devices after pairing.

Shadow Clone seal: extend the index + middle finger pair on both hands; hold one pair upright and cross the other over it. Curl ring + little fingers. Hold to summon; release, then repeat to dismiss.

To make a body-shaped clone, start the camera, step out of view, then capture the empty background.

For steadier points, keep your whole hand in view and use bright, even light. Center yourself and frame as much of your body as the camera can see; clones copy that visible area. Computer-camera frames are processed on this computer. Phone-camera mode sends live video directly to the paired computer; neither mode records or saves video.
